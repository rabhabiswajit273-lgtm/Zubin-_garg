Zubeen Garg

Singer, composer, actor and director. The voice of Assam, heard across Assamese, Hindi and Bengali music.

18 November 1972 · Tura, Meghalaya — 19 September 2025 · Singapore

Profile

Who he was, in short.

Birth nameZubeen Borthakur
Born18 Nov 1972, Tura, Meghalaya; grew up in Jorhat, Assam
Stage name"Zubeen" after conductor Zubin Mehta; "Garg" from his family gotra
ParentsMohini Mohon Borthakur and Ily Borthakur
SpouseGarima Saikia Garg, fashion designer (married 4 Feb 2002)
InstrumentsVocals, guitar, tabla, keyboard, drums, dhol
RolesPlayback singer, composer, lyricist, actor, director, scriptwriter
LanguagesSang in nearly 40 languages and dialects

He began singing at three. His mother was his first teacher, and he learned tabla for 11 years from Pandit Robin Banerjee. Guru Ramani Rai introduced him to Assamese folk. He was composing while still at school, including "Gaane Ki Aane" at 13.

Debut album Anamika in 1992 at 19. Moved to Mumbai in 1995, then returned to Assam where he became the state's most loved artist.

Legacy

He asked that "Mayabini" be sung across Assam when he was gone. People did.

Zubeen died on 19 September 2025 in Singapore, where he had gone for the North East India Festival. He was 52. His funeral in Guwahati drew millions.
